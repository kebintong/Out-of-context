On air

Twelve stages. Two bosses. One host who won't stop talking.

Inspired by I Wanna Be the Guy and Fever Meme, with a Fall Guys look. Everything that looks helpful is probably a trap.

Then the finale, the Reset Core, and the final boss. Beat the show to unlock Stage Select, Boss Rematch and Remix.

  • The Moderator

    A floating robot host who comments on every fall, lies about which door is safe, and keeps count of everything. You can poke him. He keeps count of that too.

  • Traps that troll back

    Fake floors, fake save pods, spikes that come back, platforms that dodge you, and a skip button that does not skip.

  • A quiz show

    Stand on the right answer before the timer runs out: nonsense math plus Philippine geography and history.

  • The Algorithm and MOD.EXE

    Two boss fights with ban hammers, deleted floors, lasers to jump and duck, and parries that send shots back.

  • After the credits

    Stage Select with best times, Boss Rematch with a Flawless badge, Remix mode with the traps moved, and 28 achievements.

  • Plays how you like

    Keyboard and mouse or an Xbox or PlayStation controller. Five graphics presets from Performance to Ultra, so it runs on laptops too.

Check your download

The SHA-256 checksum is a fingerprint of the file. If yours matches the one below, you have exactly the file we published, nothing changed or broken along the way.

  1. Download the game

    Save OutOfContext.exe to your Downloads folder.

  2. Open PowerShell

    Right-click the Start button and choose Terminal (or Windows PowerShell).

  3. Get the file's checksum

    Paste this and press Enter:

    Get-FileHash "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\OutOfContext.exe" -Algorithm SHA256

    Using Command Prompt instead? certutil -hashfile "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\OutOfContext.exe" SHA256

  4. Compare

    The Hash line should match the checksum on the right, letter for letter. Paste it into the box to check automatically.

What you need

It's built to run on school laptops.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Any graphics card with DirectX 11, including laptop integrated graphics
  • 4 GB of memory
  • About 200 MB of free space
  • Optional: an Xbox or PlayStation controller

Your saves stay on your PC. Uninstalling is deleting the file and the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\OutOfContext.
